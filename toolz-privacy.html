Privacy Policy

Toolz for macOS — Last updated: March 11, 2026

Toolz does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your Mac.

Overview

Toolz is a collection of productivity utilities for macOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data Toolz accesses and how it is handled.

Data Collection

Toolz collects no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No advertising or tracking
  • No network requests to our servers (the only network features are described under Third-Party Services)
  • No account or registration required

Local Data

Toolz keeps the following data locally on your Mac only:

  • Keyboard shortcuts — Your custom hotkey preferences (saved in macOS UserDefaults)
  • Clipboard history — Recent clipboard items (text and images), kept in memory while the app runs, not saved to disk, and cleared when you quit
  • Timer settings — Your focus and break duration preferences (saved in macOS UserDefaults)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Permissions

Toolz may request the following macOS permissions:

  • Screen Recording — Required by TextScanz (OCR) and ColorPikz (color picker) to capture screen content. Screen Recording is used only to capture the area you choose; captures are processed on your Mac, and files are saved only where you choose.
  • Accessibility — Required for global keyboard shortcuts to function system-wide.

Third-Party Services

The app uses only Apple's App Store services (update check and purchase verification). We use no other third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms.

Tools run on your Mac. The only network features are PingZ, which contacts a host you enter when you run it, and a check on launch against Apple's App Store for a newer version.

Children's Privacy

Toolz does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date. Since Toolz collects no data, we do not anticipate significant changes.
