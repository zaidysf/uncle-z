Privacy Policy

ToolzPad for iOS — Last updated: March 13, 2026

ToolzPad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

ToolzPad is a collection of productivity utilities for iPhone and iPad. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data ToolzPad accesses and how it is handled.

Data Collection

ToolzPad collects no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No advertising or tracking
  • No data is sent to our servers
  • No account or registration required

Local Data

ToolzPad stores the following data locally on your device only, using iOS UserDefaults:

  • Timer settings — Your focus and break duration preferences
  • App preferences — Tool-specific settings you configure

This data stays on your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Permissions

ToolzPad may request the following iOS permissions:

  • Camera — Required by ScanZ (text recognition) and QRZ (QR code scanning). Camera feed is processed locally and never transmitted.
  • Photo Library — Required by ShrinkZ to access images for compression. Images are processed locally and never uploaded.

All permissions are optional and only requested when you use the feature that requires them. You may deny any permission; only the relevant tool will be affected.

Third-Party Services

ToolzPad does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms. Your files and images are processed on your device. Only PingZ and checks with Apple's App Store services (update check and purchase verification) use the internet. PingZ sends ping requests to the host you type; the App Store checks ask Apple whether a newer version exists and verify your purchase. All other tools work without internet.

Children's Privacy

ToolzPad does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date. Since ToolzPad collects no data, we do not anticipate significant changes.
