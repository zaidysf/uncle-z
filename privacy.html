Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 2026-05-06

uncle-z studio ("uncle-z", "we", "us") is an independent product studio operated by Zaid Yasyaf in Indonesia. This policy explains what personal data we collect across uncle-z.com and the products we ship, why we collect it, and how you can ask us to change or delete it.

This policy is written in plain language because the legal version of the same content does not survive contact with reality. If you need clarification, email hello@uncle-z.com.

What we collect

On uncle-z.com itself

The marketing site is mostly static HTML. We do not run analytics or tracking scripts on it today. If that changes, this section updates first.

When you contact us or buy a service

  • Email address — when you write to hello@uncle-z.com or book a service through book.uncle-z.com.
  • Name and project context — voluntary, for whatever you brief us on.
  • Payment data — see "Payments" below.

When you use one of our products

Each product has its own scope (a POS app, a SaaS, a developer tool). What that specific product collects is documented inside the product. This page covers the umbrella; product-specific privacy notes live with the product.

Payments

Payments flow through licensed payment service providers (PSPs) — currently DOKU and NICEPAY for Indonesian rails (QRIS, Virtual Account), and Paddle / PayPal for international cards. The PSPs handle card data directly. We never see, store, or transmit your full card number.

We do receive and store: payment amount, currency, payment method (e.g. "QRIS"), the buyer's email, the PSP's transaction reference, and any metadata our products attach (e.g. an order number). This is the minimum needed to issue receipts, support refunds, and reconcile our books.

Where data is stored

Operational data lives in databases we run on servers in Singapore (closest cloud region for Indonesian latency). Backups are encrypted and stored in the same region. Some data flows transit international PSP networks (Paddle, PayPal) — this is documented in the relevant PSP's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • The PSP processing your payment (above).
  • Indonesian tax authorities (DJP) to the extent required by law.
  • Email/messaging providers strictly to send you the message you asked for (e.g. a receipt, a project update).

Your rights under UU PDP

Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU 27/2022, "UU PDP") gives you the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it's wrong.
  • Ask us to delete it (subject to tax-record retention obligations — typically 10 years for transaction records).
  • Withdraw consent for any non-essential processing.

To exercise any of these, email hello@uncle-z.com from the address tied to your account or transaction. We aim to respond within 14 days.

Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes meaningfully, we update the date at the top and post a note in the relevant product. If a change is material (e.g. new categories of data collected), we notify affected users by email.
